The Human Potential Growth Cycle

Notice today. Test this week. Learn across the cycle.

  1. Daily Separate evidence from interpretation.

    Choose one humane move and decide what success would look like.

  2. Weekly Find recurrence and contradiction.

    Invite the perspective that changes the picture and test one practice.

  3. Four-week Compare only what can honestly be compared.

    Name changing conditions, confidence, and what to carry, tune, or drop.

Build your reflection companion

AI may shorten the distance. Humans still choose the destination.

  1. 01

    Choose an AI system authorized for the exact purpose and information.

  2. 02

    Give it the public blank template or the Reader Agent Starter so it learns the method, not your private history.

  3. 03

    Keep the working journal private. Share only selected, reviewed, genuinely non-identifying reflection.

  4. 04

    Ask it to surface patterns, contradictions, missing evidence, and reversible tests.

  5. 05

    Challenge, verify, decide, and review. The human remains accountable.

Never make a completed journal public for convenience. Use only an AI system authorized for the exact purpose and document access, and provide only genuinely non-identifying, non-sensitive, policy-permitted reflection. Changing or removing a name does not make information anonymous or create permission.
