Privacy

Trust should not be the price of staying connected.

This page explains what Field Notes collects, why it is collected, and the choices that remain yours.

Field Notes and email

When you subscribe to Field Notes, Pham collects the email address you provide. This information is used to confirm your subscription, deliver the requested resource, send Field Notes, and maintain the email list.

Email subscriptions will be managed through Kit. Kit processes subscriber information on Mr Pham's behalf according to its privacy policy and data-processing terms.

Your choices

Field Notes uses confirmation by email. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. Subscriber information will not be sold or rented. You may request access, correction, or deletion by writing to matthewphamauthor@gmail.com.

Measurement

Combined statistics such as deliveries, opens, clicks, replies, downloads, and signup sources may be reviewed to understand whether the work is useful. These signals are treated as directional, not as a complete picture of any individual reader.

Human Potential Journal

Journal entries are stored only in this browser's local storage. The journal does not transmit them to Pham or an email service. They are not encrypted, and anyone who can use the same browser profile may be able to view them. Use the journal's clear control to remove saved entries from that browser. Copying or downloading creates a separate copy under your control. Anything you then share with another service is governed by that service and your organization's rules.

Keep the journal reflective and non-identifying. Do not enter learner names, nicknames, pseudonyms, traceable codes, exact class labels, identifying dates, contact details, work images, voices, confidential records, medical or safeguarding information, private communications, or other sensitive material. Changing or removing a name does not make information anonymous or create permission.

Journal text remains in local storage and is not transmitted by the journal. Ordinary visits to the website may still disclose technical information such as an IP address, browser details, timestamps, and necessary security cookies to the hosting or content-delivery provider. That technical information does not contain the text written inside the journal.

Professional boundaries

Do not submit student, family, colleague, medical, safeguarding, or other confidential information through an email signup or general contact message.
